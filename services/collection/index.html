Metal collection services

Scheduled swarf and production scrap collections for machine shops, factories and busy industrial sites, planned around your production.

Scheduled collections

Regular uplifts of swarf bins and skips, on a set day that suits production.

On-call collections

Ring when a bin is full and we collect within 24 hours.

One-off clearances

Site clear-outs, machine replacements and project surplus.

Site-specific arrangements

A collection plan built around your layout, shifts and volumes.

Containers, free of charge

We supply the right container for every job at no cost, from 10-litre tins for small volumes of precious metal waste to 40-yard roll-on/roll-off skips. Full bins are swapped quickly so production is not interrupted.

Advice on segregation

We will look at how your scrap is produced and recommend where to put bins and which grades to keep apart, so your material is worth more and stays compliant.

Coverage and practicalities

  • Depots or regions covered: e.g. London and the South East, the Midlands
  • Minimum load: if any
  • Ferrous and non-ferrous on the same visit

Paperwork
