Who we are

Ultimate Recycling Solutions (URS) designs recycling arrangements around the way each site works, with a specialist focus on scrap metal. We are based on Sunderland Road in Sandy, Bedfordshire, and run by founder Jon Fediw. Year the company was founded

Meet the boss

Jon FediwFounder

Jon has worked in the recycling business for over 20 years, from the yard floor to advising some of the UK's busiest machine shops. He started URS to give engineers and fabricators what he always wanted to see in the trade: straight answers, fair prices for their metal and collections that turn up when promised.

Call the office and there is a good chance you will be speaking to Jon himself.

Speak to Jon directly

Why independent matters

We are not tied to one processor. We place your scrap with established UK end-processors and choose the route that suits the material, which helps you get a fair price and a compliant outcome. Names or types of end-processors you work with, if you can share them

What you get

  • Free containers for every job, from 10-litre tins to 40-yard roll-on/roll-off skips
  • UK-wide collections within 24 hours
  • Prompt payment
  • Segregated swarf bins and reliable collection routines
  • All the paperwork, done for you

One contact for other waste streams
