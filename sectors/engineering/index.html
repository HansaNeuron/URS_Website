Every kind of workshop metal

Engineering firms and fabricators produce a wide mix of metal waste, from CNC swarf and plate remnants to obsolete fixtures and project surplus. We arrange collection and processing around your material grades and the way your workshop runs.

  • CNC swarf
  • Plate remnants
  • Fabrication offcuts
  • Obsolete fixtures
  • Project surplus

Segregate at the machine

Keep steel turnings, aluminium swarf and non-ferrous chips in separate bins beside each machine. Separating at source protects the value of your scrap and makes compliant recycling simpler. We will help you plan it.
