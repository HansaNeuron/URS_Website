How it works

Four steps from first call to payment.

  1. Tell us what you produce

    Metal types, rough volumes and how often. A short call or the quote form is enough.

  2. We supply the right bins, free

    Segregated bins beside your machines, sized for your volumes.

  3. We collect on your schedule

    Regular uplifts, on call, or a one-off clearance, planned around production.

  4. Weighed, graded and paid

    You get the weight tickets and paperwork, and prompt payment.

How you are paid

Non-ferrous and precious metals are worth more when they are kept clean and separated. Every collection is weighed and graded, and you are paid for what you send.

  • Pricing basis: e.g. prices linked to the LME or market rate for each grade
  • Payment terms: e.g. BACS within X working days of weighing
  • Paperwork with every collection: weight tickets and waste transfer notes assay reports, certificates of destruction
