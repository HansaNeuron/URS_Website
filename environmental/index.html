Our environmental commitment

Recycling swarf and production scrap keeps resources in use, reduces demand for newly mined metal and lowers the carbon cost of manufacturing.

Maximise material recovery

Keeping grades separate means more metal goes back into use.

Minimise environmental impact

Recycled metal needs far less energy than metal made from ore, and keeps waste out of landfill.

Stay fully compliant

Licensed carriers, permitted sites and complete duty-of-care paperwork.

Support your sustainability goals

Records you can use in your own environmental reporting.
